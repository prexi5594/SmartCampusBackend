# USIU map source and licence

The geometry in usiu-geography.geojson is derived from OpenStreetMap.
© OpenStreetMap contributors. The derivative geographic database is made available
under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
See [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The source endpoint, campus boundary ID, retrieval timestamp, per-feature OSM
IDs/versions/timestamps and limitations are recorded in the dataset and
usiu-map-sources.json. Generated review IDs are identifiers for this snapshot;
retain OSM IDs when reconciling a later extract.

The academic building/room facts in usiu-directory.json were supplied by the
owner and checked against USIU-Africa's Find Your Class v2 guide. The source URLs
are retained in that file. Classroom codes do not establish room coordinates,
floor positions, access permissions or building entrances.

The four separately sourced gate points were published for USIU-Africa's 2026
commencement. Their coordinates and everyday walking access need campus review.

No aerial imagery, photographs or map tiles are embedded. The default 8 m building
height and the conversion of OSM storeys to metres are illustrative presentation
choices, not measured campus data.
